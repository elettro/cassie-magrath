Biography

About Cassie

Cassie Magrath is a multi-talented professional actress who has performed Off-Broadway, On-Camera, and in multiple Regional Theatre productions. She has been dazzling audiences with her bubbly, outgoing personality combined with strong professionalism and work ethic. She consistently receives compliments from her directors about her preparation skills, how well she takes direction, and her ability to learn very quickly.

Cassie has been training in voice, acting and dance for most of her life. She has performed in over 40 productions. Some recent credits include Elle Woods in Legally Blonde, Matilda in Matilda the Musical, and Mary Lennox in The Secret Garden. Cassie has performed as a Hero Child in The Polar Express OKC for the past two seasons.

Cassie is currently training in voice with Ronn Burton at Lyric Thelma Gaylord Academy in OKC. She trains annually in the Summer and Winter Intensive programs with Broadway Artists Alliance in New York City, where she hones her voice, dance, and acting skills while being coached by multiple Broadway stars. BAA culminates in a live performance at a select Off-Broadway Theatre, most recently at the Alvin Ailey Theatre NYC.

Cassie was recently invited to sing at the famed New York Cabaret venue, The Green Room 42 with Spotlight Kidz. In February 2025, she became the youngest recipient ever to be awarded "Citizen of the Arts" for the City of Lawton, Oklahoma. She is currently seeking both agent and manager representation.
